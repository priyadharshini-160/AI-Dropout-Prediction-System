# 📖 Project Overview

The **AI-Based Drop-out Prediction and Counseling System** is a web-based application developed to help educational institutions identify students who may be at risk of dropping out.

The system analyzes various academic and personal factors such as attendance, CGPA, internal marks, backlogs, interest in studies, and fee status to determine the student's dropout risk level.

Based on the prediction, the application generates counseling recommendations that help students improve their academic performance and reduce the chances of dropping out.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Educational institutions often face challenges in identifying students who are likely to discontinue their education. Traditional monitoring methods are time-consuming and may overlook students who require early support.

This project provides an automated solution that predicts dropout risk and assists faculty in taking timely action through personalized counseling recommendations.

# ⚙️ How the System Works

### Step 1️⃣
Register a student by entering academic and personal details.

⬇️

### Step 2️⃣
Login to the system.

⬇️

### Step 3️⃣
The system evaluates:

- Attendance
- CGPA
- Internal Marks
- Backlogs
- Interest in Studies
- Fee Status

⬇️

### Step 4️⃣
The application calculates the student's dropout risk score.

⬇️

### Step 5️⃣
The student is classified as:

🟢 Low Risk

🟡 Medium Risk

🔴 High Risk

⬇️

### Step 6️⃣
The system displays personalized counseling recommendations.

⬇️

### Step 7️⃣
Generate and view the student's report.
